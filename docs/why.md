## What it does

Anything with a limit can be a bound. Each bound is one signed line: who said it, about what, and the range they allow. Bound takes the meet and names which of four states it is in. Nothing is true because one origin said it.

## What holds

Joining two files is putting their lines together: order does not matter, and a line twice is still one. A disagreement does not break the merge; it shows as CONFLICT. A file travels alone. Bound never guesses: one origin is REQUIRED, not FREE. Each of these is a test in bound/test/laws.test.ts.

Fold reads, land writes signed lines, sign is a key act. There is no fourth verb.

A fact is a line the CLI printed. What it did not print does not exist.

A REFUSE prints its line and stops. Nothing after it is a fact.

One lot lands one place. A lot for many places lands the same lines at each.

A second independent reader must agree. The fold rebuilds from the files alone, offline.

Joining two files is putting their lines together. Order does not matter, and a line twice is still one.

Two lines that disagree at the same epoch stand as CONFLICT. Line order does not pick a winner.

A changed byte of a signed line does not verify.

The fold is a function of its lines. Deleting a cache does not change it.

## What it refuses

No fourth verb. A batch lands whole or not at all. A bound nobody has a reading for is refused. A changed byte of a signed line does not verify. Bound does not know whether a claim is true in the world: it knows who signed what, and whether the ranges meet.

- bare: bound: REFUSE·bare nothing landed · 1 lines name a signer or an epoch they were not signed with: a drafted line proposes by=target and sign signs it, a line a lane signed carries its sig
- pin: bound: REFUSE·bare nothing landed · 1 lines name a signer or an epoch they were not signed with: a drafted line proposes by=target and sign signs it, a line a lane signed carries its sig
- place: bound: REFUSE·crash bound: REFUSE·wire `region` is not one of `wire/families`: the lock lists none
- signed: bound: REFUSE·signed nothing landed · 1 lines a lane signed stand at no epoch this act
- capture: bound: REFUSE·bare nothing landed · 2 lines name a signer or an epoch they were not signed with: a drafted line proposes by=target and sign signs it, a line a lane signed carries its sig
