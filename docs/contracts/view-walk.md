# Walk

`bound fold --as walk@<resolution>` compares the admitted place with an authenticated peer inventory. Resolution is interpreted by the declared Topos selection and projection contract; it is not restricted to three fixed numbers. The wire declares region membership, context, origin and cuts. The output names touched regions, open regions and payload bytes; protocol and imported bytes carry their own units.

Foreign history retains its signed origin epoch. Sender keys authenticate that history and never grant coverage or independent origins in the receiver. Whole-region prefixes are verified before ingress. Import and the receiver-signed local receipt commit atomically; only that receipt advances the receiver's local epoch. Foreign withdrawals are confined to the same authenticated origin and exact identifier. A repeated valid delivery adds no lines and retains its existing import receipt. A rejected or interrupted ingress adds neither evidence nor a receipt.

The published sample uses the same compiled public reader on measurement and artifact places. It compares equal summaries, opens a differing region, exchanges at intermediate declared resolutions, converges by union, retains disagreement and verifies replay after a later local act. It rejects tampered payloads, missing records, unauthorized signatures, non-prefix epochs and undeclared context changes. It interrupts a real receiver signer and verifies unchanged ingress and local history. Dead-owner recovery is a separate host action; this view does not silently recover it. The typed object's origin-local claims and withdrawals are a separate conformance vector, not inferred from configuration counts.
